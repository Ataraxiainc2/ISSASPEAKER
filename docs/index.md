# ISSASPEAKER

A personal command center for Android: your Google Home speaker, your Google account, your music and AI on your
own keys, working together in routines such as *"at 08:00, announce today's birthdays on the kitchen speaker"*.

- **Private by design:** no ISSASODA server; your data stays on your phone and in your own Google account.
  Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.html).
- **Open source:** all the code is public at
  [github.com/Ataraxiainc2/ISSASPEAKER](https://github.com/Ataraxiainc2/ISSASPEAKER) under the MIT license.
- **Security:** [security policy](https://github.com/Ataraxiainc2/ISSASPEAKER/blob/main/SECURITY.md) ·
  [threat model](security/THREAT-MODEL.html) · [audit log](security/AUDIT-LOG.html).
- **Status:** early development. [Plan](PLAN.html) · [features](FEATURES.html).
