# The owner's expanded feature list (2026-10-14)

On 2026-10-14 the owner shared a list of about 1,100 ideas in 29 sections (voice and AI, devices, PC, phone,
watch, smart home, security, music, calendar, mail, work, development, learning, health, kitchen, energy,
mobility, finances, context automations, the speaker as an operations hub, vision, memory, profiles, agents,
integrations, privacy, differentiators, technical constraints). The request: document what is useful in the
admin About (implemented / to implement), and leave out what is not worth it.

## Result

- **366 ideas kept**, rewritten as short, testable features with an English and a Spanish title.
- **16 of them already existed** in the catalog and were not added again (for example "read my own texts aloud"
  F375, "extract information from documents" F230, "calendar conflicts" F182, "generate exercises" F072).
- **350 added** as F433–F782, `origin = IDEAS`, status *To implement*.
- **About 750 ideas left out**, by section below.

By milestone: M2 daily routines 84 · M3 productivity 15 · M4 audio 10 · M5 watch and health 19 · M7 intelligence
74 · M8 experimental 36 · **M9 ecosystem (new: PC companion, Home Assistant, devices) 112**.
By priority: P1 core 59 · P2 high value 235 · P3 advanced 56.

The speaker-as-hub section (announcement queue, priority, repeat when nobody heard, history, urgent-only mode,
fallback speaker, by room, for one person) is P1 in M2: it is what makes ISSASPEAKER different from a phone app.

## What was left out, and why

| Kind | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Repeats across sections | smart plugs (sections 5 and 6), pronunciation (1 and 13), watch controls (5 and 21), agenda summaries (9, 19, 20) | Kept once |
| Device and protocol details that Home Assistant already handles | Zigbee, Z-Wave, Matter, MQTT, ESPHome management; pumps, valves, tanks, sprinklers; one feature per air sensor | ISSASPEAKER talks to Home Assistant (F-HOME) and announces; HA owns the devices |
| Constraints, not features | All of section 29 ("subject to permissions", "depends on the API") | Recorded as rules in the threat model and plan, not as features |
| Outside a phone and speaker hub | presentation and diagram generation, executive reports, KPI tracking, strategic decision registers, SQL tools, database inspection, Jira/GitLab/Node-RED, flight tracking, itineraries, currency conversion, asset tracking, 3D models | Better served by dedicated tools |
| Weak value for the effort | 14 separate "specialized agents", voice biometrics, transferring calls, posture-specific scenes (cinema, reading, holidays…) as separate features, tool and cleaning-product inventories | Replaced by general features (scenes by room, one agent framework, household profiles) |
| Risky for a public app | medical value alerts, symptom logs, automatic financial operations, unlocking doors without extra confirmation | Kept only with confirmation (smart locks), or left out |

Reconsider anything here at any time: a left-out idea becomes a feature by adding a row to
`docs/features/catalog.tsv` with `origin = IDEAS`.
